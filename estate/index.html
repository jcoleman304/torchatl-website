The estate sits east of the city in Lithonia — twenty-five minutes from the airport, far enough out that no one finds you. There is no commercial building, no shared hallway, and no other client on the property. Every reservation receives exclusive access: no other artists, no overlapping sessions.

The estate consolidates what is normally five to eight vendors — studio, lodging, meeting space, content location, catering coordination, security — into one property, one invoice, one point of contact. Bedroom to booth is a staircase.

For Labels & Management

How label teams run Torch month over month — for A&R admin, A&Rs, label presidents, managers, and product teams.

A developing act that records every week doesn't need a new quote every session. Torch runs standing monthly accounts: a block of staffed studio hours on account, drawn down by the roster, billed as one predictable line item.

Monthly accounts
Blaze — $3,000/month for 36 studio hours; built for album mode and producer rosters. The Torch — $4,500/month for 56 studio hours, with two roster artists on the account, a 30-day booking window, suites at 4 hours per night, and 20% off full-estate buyouts. A house engineer is included on every session; hours book in four-hour blocks; up to a quarter of unused hours roll to the next month. Seats are capped — two Blaze, one Torch. Application at torchatl.com/membership.
Development Partnerships
For rosters beyond membership scale: recurring residency agreements with priority access and dedicated studio time, scoped per roster. Partnerships begin where The Torch tier ends.
Admin & billing
No membership is required for label bookings — hourly sessions ($120/hr per suite, four-hour minimum, engineer included), day buyouts, and lockouts book directly on a PO. Hourly time is otherwise reserved for members. Booking requests carry label name, A&R contact, and PO number fields. One invoice per booking — or per month on account. NDAs standard for staff and sessions. Refundable deposits ($500 day / $2,500 lockout).
Rollout & content
Content wall, podcast-ready booth, camera-ready interiors — product managers and streamers run shoot days, listening sessions, and live broadcasts on the same property where the record is made.

Logistics & Policies

The advance block. What a tour manager or label coordinator needs before anyone gets in a vehicle.

Access
Gated single-entry drive; the address is never published — released on confirmed booking only. 24-hour session access for the duration of the stay: no meter, no midnight engineer curfew. Check-in 3:00 PM, early on request · check-out 11:00 AM, late if the schedule permits. Overtime is approved through management before the scheduled end time.
Occupancy
Six overnight guests across three suites; up to twelve with lounge space at the booking team's discretion. Each studio: 5 people included, 7 maximum, entering with the primary client. Event capacity is quoted per event.
Parking
Gated on-site parking for up to 15 vehicles — Sprinters and SUVs. Security-detail friendly: one perimeter to hold instead of three a day.
Privacy
NDAs available for staff and sessions. Guest registration on every entry; visitors are pre-cleared by the client's team before arrival. No location tagging — protecting unreleased records is a house rule. No public listing of guests or sessions. Tinted privacy windows — see out, no one sees in.
Cameras
Camera-monitored grounds — exterior, entry points, hallways, and common lounges only. No cameras in studio rooms, guest suites, or bathrooms.
Content
Photo and video capture is permitted across interiors and grounds; clients own what they shoot. Photoshoot wall on-site; house photographer available.
Smoking
Permitted only inside the booked studio with the door closed, on the deck's designated area, and on The Torch Suite balcony. Violations elsewhere carry a remediation fee starting at $300.
Noise
Outdoor levels are kept down late night. Studio sessions run around the clock.
