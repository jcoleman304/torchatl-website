Modes of Use

Four ways the estate runs. Every booking is exclusive — there is never another client on property.

Session

Single Session

Half-day (7 hours, from 12 PM) or full-day (12 hours). One room or both. No overnight accommodations.

Buyout

Full-Estate Lockout

Overnight buyout of the property. Both rooms running, all three suites, security included. Priced per day with multi-day tiering.

3–7+ Days

Camp / Retreat

Multi-day lockout for label, writer, and publisher camps. The roster lives, works, and records on property. Chef available. One invoice.

Event

Private Event

Listening parties, album playbacks, A&R showcases, private dinners. Gated and invite-only. Custom-quoted by scope.

Logistics & Policies

The advance block. What a tour manager or label coordinator needs before anyone gets in a vehicle.

Access
Gated single-entry drive. Exact address released on confirmed booking. 24-hour access on secured grounds for the duration of the stay. Check-in 3:00 PM · check-out 11:00 AM, extensions by availability. Overtime is approved through management before the scheduled end time.
Occupancy
Each studio: 5 people included, 7 maximum. All guests enter with the primary client. Event capacity is quoted per event.
Parking
Gated on-site parking within the estate. Oversized vehicles (Sprinter, trailer) confirmed at advance.
Connectivity
Wi-Fi throughout the estate.
Privacy
An NDA is standard on every booking. No public listing of guests or sessions. Tinted privacy windows — see out, no one sees in.
Cameras
Security cameras cover exterior, entry points, hallways, and common lounges only. No cameras in studio rooms, guest suites, or bathrooms.
Content
Photo and video capture is permitted across interiors and grounds; clients own what they shoot. Photoshoot wall on-site; house photographer available.
Smoking
Permitted only inside the booked studio with the door closed, on the deck's designated area, and on the Presidential balcony. Violations elsewhere carry a remediation fee starting at $300.
Noise
Outdoor levels are kept down late night. Studio sessions run around the clock.
